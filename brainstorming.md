**Mobile:**
This mobile app would be somewhat unique, while most journals are physical and this is not. There are other journal apps online. **Story:**
Once this story is completed, people may feel compelled to want an accessible pocket journal to record current events, daily thoughts, and mood tracking. For indiviuals who like to journal, this app is a very valuable tool. As it is enviormentlly friendly, convenient, and easily accessible. The responses to this product idea would vary on the person. Some people are very intuitive with their thoughts or feelings, while some people don't prefer to journal. 

**Market:** How large or unique is the market for this app?

Yes this app can provide a huge value to those niche group of people. To those who journal and may not want to carry around a physical journal, or for those who want to journal but are more enviormentally concious this would be great for them. This app would mostly be targeted to young to middle aged women, as a higher percentage of women journal than men. This would also be targeted to those who those who use it for health purposes or as a health indicator. 

**Habit:** This app could be very habit-forming for people who like to journal. People may feel compelled to journal before going on social media, calling friends, or playing a game. It would be recommended for an average user to open this app pretty frequently or at least once a day. The average user would use this app to "create" something, and they would be creating journal entries and eventually an entire journal.

**Scope:**
The scope of this app is clear and can can be realistic for the average user. It is manageable and could potentially be measureable.Since this app is mostly based on user-entry, it won't be too hard to complete by the deadline. It would just be a big learning-curve figuring out how to implement all the features we want. A stripped down version of this app still interesting to build considering the colors, layout, and features we want to incorportate.
