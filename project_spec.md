## App Overview

### Description 

**Welly is a wellness app designed for those who like to journal, and want to do it from the convience of their phone. It allows for the user to enter and keep track of different journal entries day by day.**

### App Evaluation

<!-- Evaluation of your app across the following attributes -->

**Mobile:**
This mobile app would be somewhat unique, while most journals are physical and this is not. There are other journal apps online. With this being an app, it makes it more accessible than if it was a website. This is because you have your phone on you at all times, and you can easily open the app with a click of a button and make an entry.

**Story:**
Once this story is completed, people may feel compelled to want an accessible pocket journal to record current events, daily thoughts, and mood tracking. For indiviuals who like to journal, this app is a very valuable tool. As it is enviormentlly friendly, convenient, and easily accessible. The responses to this product idea would vary on the person. Some people are very intuitive with their thoughts or feelings, while some people don't prefer to journal. 

**Market:** How large or unique is the market for this app?
Journaling isn't a very popular activity, with only 11% of the population participating daily, according to gemini. So it's a very niche market, in accordance to those stats. Yes this app can provide a huge value to those niche group of people. To those who journal and may not want to carry around a physical journal, or for those who want to journal but are more enviormentally concious this would be great for them. This app would mostly be targeted to young to middle aged women, as a higher percentage of women journal than men. This would also be targeted to those who those who use it for health purposes or as a health indicator. 

**Habit:** This app could be very habit-forming for people who like to journal. People may feel compelled to journal before going on social media, calling friends, or playing a game. It would be recommended for an average user to open this app pretty frequently or at least once a day. The average user would use this app to "create" something, and they would be creating journal entries and eventually an entire journal.

**Scope:**
The scope of this app is clear and can can be realistic for the average user. It is manageable and could potentially be measureable.Since this app is mostly based on user-entry, it won't be too hard to complete by the deadline. It would just be a big learning-curve figuring out how to implement all the features we want. A stripped down version of this app still interesting to build considering the colors, layout, and features we want to incorportate. The product we want to build is clearly defined as a journal entry app.


## Product Spec

### 1. User Features (Required and Optional)

Required Features:

- Create entries
- View past entries
- Edit/update entries
- Calender 
- Adding photos 

Stretch Features:

- Creating an account
- Mood tracker

### 2. Chosen API(s)

- **http://api.forismatic.com/api/1.0/**

### 3. User Interaction

Required Feature

- **Creating an account**
  - Username // password log-in
  - Or signing in exisiting account
  - ...
- **Selecting a date**
  - Picking a date from the calender to edit
  - Other months, year, weeks, etc. avaliable to view
- **Creating an entry**
    - After selecting a day, users can add/edit/update journal entry 
    - Saves progress throughout all entries
- **Adding photos**
    - Users are able to add pictures within their journal entries and can edit/update as well
    - Saves progress throughout all entries
